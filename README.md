### About this repository

This repository contains interactive 3D visualizations of the detonation front structure, provided as supplementary material for the paper listed above. The figures can be rotated, zoomed in, and zoomed out to better understand the complex three-dimensional detonation structure — something not possible in static print figures.

The simulations were performed in a square channel accommodating half a detonation cell across its width for a 2H₂+O₂+2Ar mixture, using a 3D Euler solver with adaptive mesh refinement and a tuned two-step reaction model.

---

### Figures

**Fig. S1** — A three-dimensional numerical soot foil obtained by tracking triple lines at each time step and following their motion over time. These trajectories outline the detonation cell boundaries, analogous to triple point trajectories on a classical soot foil.

**Fig. S2** (*t* = 44.75) — Detonation structure represented with the shock front followed by iso-surfaces of *Y*r = 0.1 and *Y*r = 0.95. The transverse waves are reflected from the side walls, while the oblique shock is about to reflect at the corner.

**Fig. S3** (*t* = 46) — Detonation structure represented with the shock front followed by iso-surfaces of *Y*r = 0.1 and *Y*r = 0.95. The oblique shock has just reflected from the corner.

**Fig. S4** (*t* = 48.5) — Detonation structure represented with the shock front followed by iso-surfaces of *Y*r = 0.1 and *Y*r = 0.95. The transverse waves are located approximately in the middle of the channel. This figure corresponds to Fig. 3, Fig. 5, and Fig. 6 in the paper.

Figs. S2–S4 show the shock surface (teal) and two iso-surfaces of the reaction zone progress variable *Y*r located behind the shock front, where *Y*r = 0 means reactions have not yet started and *Y*r = 1 means reactions are complete.
